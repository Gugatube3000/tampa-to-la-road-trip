Overview

A consistent finish for every outside corner.

Novex Cornerboards combine both faces in one profile, bringing a consistent edge to the transition between siding elevations.

Compare face widths, lengths and finishes before choosing the profile for a wall corner, bay or dormer.

  • One-piece outside corner profile
  • Smooth outer edges
  • One profile for both faces of the corner
  • Cellular PVC for exterior trim applications
  • Matte-white finish with selected woodgrain options
  • Same look and feel as Novex Trim
Ideal for
  • Outside wall corners
  • Bays & bump-outs
  • Dormers
  • Garages & outbuildings

Finishes

Find the finish that suits the home.

Compare smooth and woodgrain finishes in matte white. Options vary by product and size; ask about samples before selecting.

Smooth

Smooth

A smooth, semi-matte white surface with the look of freshly painted, clear-grade trim. A clean finish for crisp architectural lines.

Woodgrain

Woodgrain

A rustic, embossed woodgrain that reads as traditional painted wood, even up close. A textured option for traditional exterior detailing.

Installation & care

Good to know.

The essentials for a clean, lasting installation — and answers to the questions we hear most. Our team is always happy to talk through your project.

Contact our team
Before installation

Confirm the dimensions, substrate and application before work begins. Request the current installation instructions for your product, and review local building requirements with your installer.

Fastening and joints

Use the product's installation instructions to determine fasteners, spacing and joint details for the application. Ask our team for the relevant documentation before choosing a fastening system.

Choosing a finish

Compare the smooth and woodgrain options available for your chosen size. If you plan to paint, confirm the coating and color requirements in the product and paint manufacturer's guidance.

Care and maintenance

Follow the product's care instructions. Ask for guidance before using a cleaning chemical, abrasive tool or pressure washer on a finished surface.
