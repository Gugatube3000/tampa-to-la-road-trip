About Novex

Rooted in Boston. Built for New England.

Novex Exterior supplies premium PVC trim to the people who build, restore and care for New England homes.

Our story

Details that endure.

New England homes are defined by their details: the crisp corner of a colonial, the deep cornice of a triple-decker, the pediment over a Greek Revival door.

For generations those details were made of wood — and for generations, homeowners have scraped, patched and replaced them. Novex Exterior exists to end that cycle. We supply cellular PVC trim boards, sheets and cornerboards that carry the proportions and profiles of traditional millwork, engineered to stand up to the coastline and the winters that come with living here.

We’re a Boston company. We know the neighborhoods, the building stock and the people who work on it — and we’ve built our business around making their jobs easier: the right sizes on hand, honest advice and deliveries that arrive when promised.

What we stand for

Four principles.

I.

Craft

We care about proportions, profiles and shadow lines as much as the carpenters who install them.

II.

Durability

Every product we sell is chosen to outlast the weather it will face — here, that means a lot.

III.

Service

Real people, quick answers and deliveries you can plan a crew around.

IV.

Integrity

Straight talk on sizes, lead times and pricing. No surprises, ever.
