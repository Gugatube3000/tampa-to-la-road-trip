Novex Sheets

Ease and efficiency in wide applications. For anything over 16″ wide, cover the area with a single sheet instead of piecing together boards — and save time and money on installation.

Overview

When a detail is wider than a board, reach for a sheet.

Novex Sheets leave the constant maintenance of wood behind, with the same look, feel and lasting performance as Novex Trim — in a single, four-foot-wide piece.

Use them for bay windows, dormers, gables, soffits and raised panels, or rip them into custom widths for wraps and millwork.

  • Covers a wide area with a single piece
  • Stands up to harsh weather and UV, inside and out
  • Resists stains, scratches and fading
  • Resists mold, mildew and moisture damage
  • No cupping, rotting or splitting
  • Suitable for ground-contact applications
Ideal for
  • Bay windows
  • Dormers
  • Gable fields & pediments
  • Soffits & porch ceilings
  • Raised panels
  • Column & beam wraps

Installation & care

Good to know.

The essentials for a clean, lasting installation — and answers to the questions we hear most. Our team is always happy to talk through your project.

Talk to our team
Fastening

Use stainless steel or hot-dip galvanized fasteners rated for PVC trim, long enough to penetrate at least 1½″ into solid framing.

  • Two fasteners every 16″ along the length for boards up to 12″ wide; add a fastener for wider boards and sheets.
  • Keep fasteners within 2″ of board ends and at least ¾″ from edges.
  • Set heads just below the surface and fill with a PVC-compatible filler for an invisible finish.
Expansion & contraction

Cellular PVC moves with temperature, not moisture. Plan joints so that movement is controlled and invisible.

  • Allow roughly ⅛″ of movement for every 18′ of length.
  • Bond scarf and miter joints with PVC cement and fasten both sides of the joint.
  • Securely fasten long runs so expansion is distributed along the board instead of at the ends.
Cutting, routing & milling

Novex products work like a premium softwood using standard carpentry tools.

  • Use carbide-tipped blades and bits for the cleanest edges.
  • Rout decorative edges, mill custom profiles and rip sheets to width on site.
  • Heat-forming with the right equipment allows curves and arches.
Painting (optional)

Paint isn’t required for protection, but it’s easy when you want color.

  • Use a 100% acrylic latex exterior paint; no primer is required on clean surfaces.
  • For darker colors, choose a paint formulated for vinyl or PVC to limit heat build-up — or stay with colors of LRV 55 or higher.
  • Clean surfaces of dust and handling marks before painting.
Do I need to paint it?

No. Novex products don’t need paint for protection, but you can paint them for a custom color or to cover filled nail holes. Use a 100% acrylic latex exterior paint with an LRV of 55 or higher.

Does it come in colors?

Novex trim, sheets and cornerboards are made in a matte white only, and they take paint easily when you want a custom color.

How do I clean it?

Hose off loose dirt, or use a soft cloth with a mild detergent. If you use a power washer, test the pressure and nozzle on an inconspicuous spot first so the surface isn’t damaged.

General guidance only. Always follow the installation instructions supplied with your product, local building codes and the recommendations of your fastener and paint manufacturers.
