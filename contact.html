FAQ

Good questions.

Do you sell to homeowners, or only to contractors?

Both. Contractors, builders and dealers can open a trade account for volume pricing and scheduled delivery. Homeowners are welcome to order directly — we’re happy to help plan materials and can point you toward experienced local installers.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver to jobsites throughout Greater Boston and across New England. Lead times depend on order size and location — tell us about your project and we’ll confirm a delivery window.

Does Novex trim need to be painted?

No. Novex products don’t need paint for protection and arrive in a clean matte white. If you want color, use a 100% acrylic latex exterior paint; for darker colors choose a paint formulated for vinyl or PVC.

Can I get samples?

Absolutely. Request a finish sample kit with our smooth and cedar-grain finishes using the form above, or ask your account contact.

How does PVC trim compare to wood?

It looks like premium painted wood and works with the same tools, but it won’t absorb water — so it won’t rot, split, cup or attract insects, and it doesn’t need regular repainting to stay protected.

Can you help with quantities?

Yes. Send plans, elevations or a rough list and we’ll prepare a takeoff with sizes, lengths and quantities, plus a quote.
