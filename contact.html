FAQ

Common questions.

Which product do I need?

Start with the application: trim boards for narrow details, sheets for wider panels and one-piece cornerboards for outside corners. Share your dimensions or drawings so we can discuss the options.

What should I include in a quote request?

Include the product, dimensions, quantities, finish, project location and timeline. If you are still planning, describe the detail or send a material list.

Can I ask about samples?

Choose “Finish samples” in the form and tell us which product and finish you want to compare. Our team can confirm the samples available.

How do I confirm availability and delivery?

Tell us your project location, required sizes, quantities and timeline. Confirm current stock, lead time and delivery arrangements before placing an order.
