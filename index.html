Performance

Engineered for the Northeast.

Wood trim fails where New England is hardest on it — at the joints, ends and edges where water lingers. Novex cellular PVC doesn’t absorb moisture, so there’s nothing to rot, swell or come apart.

Moisture-proof core

Won’t absorb water, so it won’t rot, swell or cup.

Insect resistant

No wood fiber for carpenter ants or termites.

Freeze–thaw stable

No checking, splitting or popped joints after a hard winter.

Coastal ready

Unbothered by salt air, from the North Shore to the Cape.

Paint optional

Ships in a clean matte white. Paint it — or don’t.

Works like wood

Cuts, routes, mills and fastens with standard tools.

Finishes

Two finishes. Both flawless.

Choose the crisp, contemporary look of a smooth face or the traditional character of an embossed grain. Most Novex boards are reversible, so you can decide on site.
