Performance

Engineered for the Northeast.

Wood trim fails where New England is hardest on it — at the joints, ends and edges where water lingers. Novex cellular PVC doesn’t absorb moisture, so there’s nothing to rot, swell or come apart.

Moisture-proof core

Resists moisture, mold and mildew. No cupping, rotting or splitting.

Insect resistant

No wood fiber for carpenter ants or termites.

Weather tough

Stands up to nor’easters, freeze–thaw and UV, inside and out.

Ground-contact rated

Suitable down at the ground line and along the coast.

Paint optional

A clean matte white that resists stains, scratches and fading.

Works like wood

Mill, rout and heat-form it with standard tools.
