Trim Boards

Overview

Everything you love about clear wood. None of what you don’t.

Novex Trim Boards are made from cellular PVC with a dense, uniform structure that cuts, routes and fastens like premium clear lumber — without knots, checks or the slow creep of rot.

Use them anywhere you would use painted wood. The result is the same crisp, traditional look on day one, and a trim package that still looks that way decades later.

  • Won’t rot, swell, cup or split from moisture
  • Consistent, knot-free boards in every bundle
  • Reversible: smooth on one face, cedar grain on the other
  • Holds a crisp edge for routing and custom profiles
  • Paint-ready, but never needs paint for protection
  • Long lengths for fewer joints on rakes and fascia

Installation

Install it right.

The essentials for a clean, lasting installation. Our team is always happy to talk through the details of your project.

Talk to our team
Fastening

Use stainless steel or hot-dip galvanized fasteners rated for PVC trim, long enough to penetrate at least 1½″ into solid framing.

  • Two fasteners every 16″ along the length for boards up to 12″ wide; add a fastener for wider boards and sheets.
  • Keep fasteners within 2″ of board ends and at least ¾″ from edges.
  • Set heads just below the surface and fill with a PVC-compatible filler for an invisible finish.
Expansion & contraction

Cellular PVC moves with temperature, not moisture. Plan joints so that movement is controlled and invisible.

  • Allow roughly ⅛″ of movement for every 18′ of length.
  • Bond scarf and miter joints with PVC cement and fasten both sides of the joint.
  • Securely fasten long runs so expansion is distributed along the board instead of at the ends.
Cutting, routing & milling

Novex products work like a premium softwood using standard carpentry tools.

  • Use carbide-tipped blades and bits for the cleanest edges.
  • Rout decorative edges, mill custom profiles and rip sheets to width on site.
  • Heat-forming with the right equipment allows curves and arches.
Painting (optional)

Paint isn’t required for protection, but it’s easy when you want color.

  • Use a 100% acrylic latex exterior paint; no primer is required on clean surfaces.
  • For darker colors, choose a paint formulated for vinyl or PVC to limit heat build-up — or stay with colors of LRV 55 or higher.
  • Clean surfaces of dust and handling marks before painting.
Care & cleaning

Keep it looking new with occasional cleaning.

  • Wash with mild soap and water and a soft brush.
  • Avoid solvents and abrasive cleaners on unpainted surfaces.

General guidance only. Always follow the installation instructions supplied with your product, local building codes and the recommendations of your fastener and paint manufacturers.
