Overview

Trim for windows, doors and rooflines.

Novex Trim brings a consistent cellular PVC material to non-structural exterior details. Use standard profiles or discuss the requirements for a custom detail with your installer.

Choose smooth or woodgrain in matte white. If you plan to paint, check the product and paint manufacturer's guidance before selecting a coating and color.

  • Cellular PVC for non-structural exterior applications
  • Consistent, knot-free boards in every bundle
  • Suitable for a range of custom exterior details
  • Protective film for storage and handling
  • Paint optional — matte white out of the box
  • Long lengths for fewer joints on rakes and fascia
Ideal for
  • Window & door casings
  • Fascia & frieze
  • Rake boards
  • Skirt & water table
  • Band boards
  • Custom millwork
